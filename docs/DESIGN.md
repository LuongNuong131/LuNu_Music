# LuNu listening room

A personal music player, built around Vietnamese music, album artwork and a quiet listening experience.

Palette: midnight `#111526`, ink `#191e33`, lavender `#b6a1ff`, periwinkle `#d9d0ff`, cloud `#f5f6fc`, coral `#ffb5a7`. Dark and light themes share the same hierarchy. Be Vietnam Pro is self-hosted for reliable Vietnamese text.

Layout: left navigation, a flexible music workspace, a small now-playing column on wide screens, and a persistent player. On phones the music comes first; navigation becomes a drawer. All headings align left.

```
navigation | greeting / quick tools
           | featured listening mix | now playing
           | album rotation         | up next
           | searchable library     |
           | persistent player across the bottom
```

The distinctive moment is a lavender record sleeve with an oversized vinyl illustration. Everything around it stays restrained. Album covers, song titles and real listening history supply the content; no invented popularity or audio-quality claims. Controls use consistent SVG icons. Motion responds to playback and respects reduced motion.

Review: an initial all-dark, glowing dashboard would repeat the previous UI. The record sleeve instead introduces a clear physical music reference and a contrasting surface. Large type uses one Vietnamese family, without italic or colored-word headlines. Components have different treatments according to their job, rather than repeating identical cards.
